Get involved

Become a volunteer, member or donor. Whatever time you have, you can make a difference for a young person.

Send sign-upSupport us

Choose how to contribute

  • Chat volunteerOne evening a week, from home. You get training and supervision.
  • Group leaderHelp run group meetings and activities in one of our cities.
  • MemberSupport the association and have a say at the annual meeting. SEK 100 a year.
  • Monthly donorGive a fixed amount each month so we can plan long term.

How to become a volunteer

  1. 1

    Sign up

    Fill in the form below. It takes five minutes.

  2. 2

    Conversation

    We'll call you for a short chat about you and the role.

  3. 3

    Training

    Three evenings on conversation skills, confidentiality and self-care.

  4. 4

    Supervision

    You get regular supervision and always have a colleague to turn to.

You need to be at least 20, have around four hours a week and be able to show a police record extract.
