For schools and municipalities

We share what we learn from thousands of conversations with young people. Talks, training and process support for schools, social services and municipalities.

Send a booking request

What we offer

Our trainers do support conversations every week themselves. Everything we teach builds on real experience, research and young people's own voices – tailored to your organisation.

  • Early signs – young people struggling at home

    How do you notice a student at risk? We cover warning signs, how to ask, and what the duty to report means in practice.

    Audience
    Teachers, student health, youth workers
    Format
    Talk, on site or online
    Length
    90 minutes
    Price
    From SEK 8,500

  • The difficult conversation

    Practical tools for talking with young people about violence, self-harm and worries at home – without closing the door.

    Audience
    School staff and social services
    Format
    Half-day training with exercises
    Length
    3.5 hours
    Price
    From SEK 18,000

  • Student talk: You are not alone

    A young person with lived experience speaks alongside one of our staff. About asking for help, and where to find it.

    Audience
    Students aged 13–19
    Format
    Talk in classroom or hall
    Length
    60 minutes
    Price
    From SEK 6,000

  • Process support for municipalities

    We help you build open, accessible support services for young people – with young people's voices throughout.

    Audience
    Social services, departments, politicians
    Format
    Workshop series, tailored
    Length
    3–6 sessions
    Price
    On quotation
