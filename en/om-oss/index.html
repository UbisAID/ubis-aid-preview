About us

We exist so that no young person has to be alone with what's hard. We offer open, free and anonymous support to children and young people aged 13–25.

Our mission

  • Vision

    A society where every young person has at least one adult who listens.

  • Mission

    We provide open social support to young people who are struggling – through conversations, groups and activities – and share knowledge with the adults who meet them.

  • How we work

    Low threshold and on young people's terms. No requirements, no queues, no records. Young people's own voices guide how we develop.

Our values

  • We listen without judging
  • The young person sets the pace
  • Safety comes first
  • We're honest – even when it's hard
