What you tell us stays with us. Only if your life or someone else's is in danger do we contact others – and we tell you first.

How it works

  1. 1

    You reach out

    Write or call when it suits you. You don't have to give your name or explain everything at once.

  2. 2

    A support person replies

    Everyone who replies is trained and supervised. Sometimes you'll wait a moment – we answer as soon as we can.

  3. 3

    You talk at your pace

    You decide what to talk about. You can stop whenever you like and come back another day.
